Privacy policy

QRetina, for Android and iOS. Effective 27 September 2026.

In short

QRetina collects no personal data, has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and makes no network connections. Everything it handles stays on your phone unless you choose to send it.

Camera

The camera is used only while the Receive screen is open, to read QR codes. Camera images are processed on the phone and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Files and photos

QRetina reads a file or photo only when you choose it for broadcasting, or share it to QRetina from another app. Items you receive or broadcast are kept in QRetina's own storage on your phone, until you delete them or share them with another app yourself.

Broadcasts

A broadcast is shown on your screen as QR codes. Anyone who can see the screen can record it. Broadcasts are encoded so that other QR readers cannot read them, but that is not a security measure: use a group key to make a broadcast private to the people who hold that key.

Keys and settings

Group keys are kept in your phone's secure storage (the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore). Settings, approvals of programs and the list of revoked publishers are kept in QRetina's storage on your phone.

Programs

Programs you receive and choose to run are isolated from your data and from the network. They cannot read your files, your other items or your keys.

Deleting your data

Delete items in the Library, or uninstall QRetina to remove everything it stored.

Children

QRetina collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page. Questions: github.com/lucas-ebi/qretina/issues.
