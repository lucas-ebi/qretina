QRetina

QRetina sends files between phones as a stream of QR codes: one phone shows them, the other films them. It needs no network, pairing or radio, and works when frames are missed.

You scanned a QRetina broadcast, but the app is not installed on this phone.

  • Android (APK)
  • App Store and Google Play: not yet published.

Once installed, scan the broadcast again: the countdown at its start opens QRetina.
